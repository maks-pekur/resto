NOT a stack-walker — call-site enforcement remains an ESLint job (TEN-12).

4. **TEN-15** — Two `no-restricted-syntax` selectors in `packages/config-eslint/base.mjs`:
   - `Property[key.name='correlationId'] > CallExpression[callee.name='randomUUID']`
   - `Property[key.name='correlationId'] > CallExpression[callee.object.name='crypto'][callee.property.name='randomUUID']`
     Both reject direct construction; the only sanctioned source for `correlationId` is `buildEnvelope()` from `@resto/events` (TEN-14, PR 3).
