<objective>
Collapse the API to one apex parameter and make every guest-facing URL it emits land on the
surface that actually serves it.

Purpose: three of these URLs are printed, emailed or stored — a QR sticker, an order-confirmation
link, and a `tenant_domains` row the operator's own settings page renders as their menu address.
Each is wrong today under the single-apex scheme, and each is wrong in a way nobody notices until
a guest is standing at a table or a prospect is reading an email. Getting them right is cheaper
now than correcting rows by hand later.

Output: `GUEST_APEX_DOMAIN` deleted from the codebase, one shared helper composing every
guest-facing URL, and two tenancy writers that stop hardcoding a domain the founder does not own.
</objective>
