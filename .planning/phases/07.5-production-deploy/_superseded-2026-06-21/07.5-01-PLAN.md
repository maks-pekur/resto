<objective>
GATING SPIKE: Decide the managed-Postgres provider (Neon vs RDS) by proving — with a live
psql test against a real throwaway Neon project — whether RestOS's `resto_auth` role can be
granted `BYPASSRLS` by a `neon_superuser` member (`neondb_owner`). This is D-04's HARD GATE:
the 3-role + FORCE-RLS model is the product's core tenant-isolation defensibility, and
`provisionAuthRole()` hard-fails boot if `BYPASSRLS` does not land on `resto_auth`.

Purpose: Everything provider-specific downstream (DB provisioning, two connection strings,
backup/restore strategy, secrets) depends on this single verdict. Running it first prevents
committing the plan to Neon and discovering at provisioning time that the model is inexpressible.
Output: `DB-PROVIDER-DECISION.md` recording the verdict, the psql evidence, and the exact
pooled/direct/admin connection-string plan for the chosen provider.
</objective>
