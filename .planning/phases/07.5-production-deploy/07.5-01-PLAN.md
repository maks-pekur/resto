<objective>
Resolve the D-04 HARD GATE before any infrastructure is provisioned: prove on a real throwaway database that the chosen managed-Postgres provider can express RestOS's 3-role model — `resto_app` (NOBYPASSRLS), the schema-owner/admin-migration role (CREATEROLE + DDL + CREATE EXTENSION), and `resto_auth` (BYPASSRLS) — together with `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` and the `pgcrypto` / `citext` / `pg_trgm` extensions.

Purpose: The 3-role + FORCE-RLS model is the product's core tenant-isolation defensibility. Both persona reviews (CTO "[HIGH on provider capability]", Investor "BLOCK #1") flagged that managed Postgres restricts `BYPASSRLS` in vendor-specific ways (Neon ties it to `neon_superuser`; RDS gates it behind `rds_superuser`). If the provider cannot express the role separation, picking it would silently compromise isolation in prod. No downstream provisioning plan (06) may run until this decision is recorded with proof.

Output: `.planning/phases/07.5-production-deploy/DB-PROVIDER-DECISION.md` — a decision record naming the provider, embedding the spike transcript, and documenting the connection-model split (pooled app path vs. direct session-pinned dispatcher connection — D-05).
</objective>
