**Gathered:** 2026-09-02
**Status:** Ready for planning

> **READ THIS FIRST.** `.planning/notes/ingredients.md` is the design source named by ROADMAP.md,

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

An ingredient becomes an entity of the restaurant rather than a row inside one group: photo, name,
description, price, entered once and reused. Membership moves into link tables; a dish can also
carry single ingredients directly. A dish's **composition** can be assembled from those same
ingredients, and a composition line marked removable lets the guest cross it out — the exclusion
reaches the order as its own rows. Ingredients get their own per-location stop list. The admin gets
an Ingredients screen; the guest gets an explicit per-group display (tiles or tabs).

**Not in this phase:** amounts above one and the stepper, pre-selected ("default-in") ingredients,
the guest's remembered dislikes, price per size, per-dish group rules, gram-and-cost assembly
charts, nested modifiers.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### The ingredient and its attachments

- **D-01:** The Ingredients screen holds **identity only** — photo, name, description, price. Nothing
  about behaviour lives there.
- **D-02:** Behavioural fields belong on the **attachment** (the group→ingredient or dish→ingredient
  link), not on the ingredient. This is a locked principle for later phases (the stepper, price per
  size). In *this* phase it costs nothing: with default-in removed (D-05) and the stepper deferred
  (D-06), the attachment carries only `sort_order`. Do not add nullable behaviour columns "for
  later" — add them in the phase that uses them.
- **D-03:** Price is global to the ingredient. One bacon in three groups is one payload entry and one
  price. (Carried from ROADMAP, reconfirmed.)
- **D-04:** An ingredient attaches to a dish **exactly once**. The union of the dish's groups and its
  single ingredients must be duplicate-free; the admin refuses the second attachment and names where
  it already appears. No dedupe at order time, no two rows for one ingredient on one dish.
- **D-05:** **Default-in is out of this phase entirely.** No pre-selected ingredients, no `amount: 0`,
  no "без лука" through modifiers. A guest only ever *adds* a modifier. Removal is expressed through
  the composition instead (D-14). ING-06 moves to the backlog.
- **D-06:** **Amounts above one and the stepper are deferred.** Every chosen ingredient is taken once;
  `amount` is always 1. ING-07 moves to the backlog. "Double cheese" is a separate ingredient until
  the stepper ships.

### Group shape and behaviour

- **D-07:** Every group is configured the same way, with **two explicit fields plus required**:
  **display** (tiles | tabs) and **behaviour** (one | several), and `is_required`. The operator never
  types a number. This **replaces** the note's "shape comes from photos, behaviour comes from
  effective max" rule and the numeric `min_selectable` / `max_selectable` in the admin.
- **D-08:** There are exactly **two display variants — tiles and tabs**. The checkbox-row rendering
  disappears from the guest UI; a photoless group renders as tiles.
- **D-09:** A tile whose ingredient has no photo shows a **placeholder in the photo slot**, so a mixed
  block keeps an even row.
- **D-10:** The ingredient's description is shown to the guest as a **small line under the name** on
  the tile, single line, ellipsised.
- **D-11:** Existing groups migrate by their current numbers, no operator action: `max_selectable = 1`
  → **tabs + one**; `max_selectable > 1` → **tiles + several**; `is_required` carries across as-is.
  The two dev groups (dough, sauce) land on tabs + one + required.
- **D-12:** Behaviour and display are set on the **group**, and therefore apply identically to every
  dish that uses that group. A dish that needs something different attaches the ingredient singly.
  No per-dish override.

### Composition, and the guest crossing an ingredient out

- **D-13:** `menu_items.ingredients` is renamed to `menu_items.composition` and moves out of
  `item-modifier-groups-card.tsx` into the item's description form.
- **D-14:** Composition can be authored **two ways, both in this phase**: as free text (what exists
  today) or **assembled from ingredients**. The operator picks the mode per dish.
- **D-15:** An assembled composition is an **ordered list of ingredients — no grams, no units, no
  cost**. Assembly charts stay out (they are iiko's domain and need stock first).
- **D-16:** Each assembled composition line carries one property: **removable or not**. Not removable
  → it reads as plain text to the guest. Removable → the guest sees it with a cross; tapping strikes
  it through, which means "leave it out".
- **D-17:** The composition is **visible to the guest as a line under the dish description** — text,
  not photo cards, so it never competes with the add-ons block.
- **D-18:** An excluded ingredient reaches the order as **its own rows on the order line**, with the
  name snapshotted the way modifier rows already are. **Nothing goes into the order comment** — the
  phase rule holds. Text in a comment can be neither counted nor handed to a POS.
- **D-19:** The operator sees exclusions as a **separate line in the order detail sheet**, beside the
  modifier list (`order-detail-sheet.tsx:320`). The feed card renders no modifiers today and is not
  changed. _(Claude's call — see Discretion.)_

### Stop list

- **D-20:** **Only the operator puts anything in the stop list.** There is no automatic cascade from
  a stopped ingredient to the dishes that use it.
- **D-21:** A stopped ingredient reaches the guest through the existing `/v1/menu/availability`
  response as a **second list beside `stoppedItemIds`**; the guest renders it greyed with a
  "закончился" label, exactly as `menu-screen.tsx:300` already does for items. **The published menu
  omits nothing** — the note's "a stopped ingredient is omitted from the published menu" is wrong
  about how stops work today.
- **D-22:** When the operator stops an ingredient, the admin lists the dishes whose **composition**
  contains it, with checkboxes, and offers to stop them too. The operator decides and may stop none.
  The list is built from the assembled composition (D-14), not from modifier attachments — being an
  add-on is not being part of the dish.
- **D-23:** The stop overlay is **computed per read** inside `loadPublishedMenu`, not materialised at
  publish. _(Claude's call — see Discretion.)_
- **D-24:** An order carrying a stopped ingredient is **rejected with its own error code**, the same
  way a stopped dish is rejected, so the guest UI can name what ran out.
- **D-25:** **Known consequence, accepted:** with no cascade, a dish whose required block has every
  option stopped stays in the menu and looks orderable until opened; the Add button is then inactive
  through the existing unmet-group check. This is the price of "only the operator decides" and is not
  a bug to fix in this phase.

### Admin surface

- **D-26:** The Menu section keeps **three top-level screens**. Ingredients is one screen holding two
  inner tabs: the ingredients themselves and the groups. "Группы модификаторов" is renamed to
  "Группы ингредиентов" and moves inside it.
- **D-27:** The ingredients list is a **plain card grid — no search, no paging**. A restaurant has
  tens, not hundreds. Add search when it gets crowded.
- **D-28:** Archiving an ingredient that is in use shows **a warning listing every group and dish it
  appears in**; on confirmation it is removed from those places and archived.
- **D-29:** Permissions follow the **menu resource** — whoever edits dishes edits ingredients. No new
  RBAC resource, no role re-sync for existing tenants (unlike the `table` resource in 10.3).

### Migration and data

- **D-30:** The **dev database is reset**, not backfilled. No QR sheets have been printed, so the
  10.2 D-12 dev-reset allowance is still open (10.4's note: printing is what closes it). Present data
  is 3 options, 2 groups, 6 dish→group links, 0 `order_modifiers`, 40 items. A real backfill is not
  written in this phase; the reset also clears 873 test-residue tenants.
- **D-31:** The migration is **hand-written**. `drizzle-kit generate` still emits a whole-schema
  recreation because `meta/0000_snapshot.json` was lost in the 10.2 squash — see
  `.planning/todos/pending/drizzle-generate-is-broken.md`. Latest migration is
  `0018_menu_item_diets.sql`; this phase starts at `0019`.
- **D-32:** Both new link tables carry `tenant_id`, composite tenant FKs to both parents (ADR-0020
  I-2) and lead every index with `tenant_id`, matching `menu_item_modifier_groups`.

### Claude's Discretion

- **D-19** (exclusions render in the order detail sheet, not the feed card) — decided from the code:
  the feed card shows no modifiers at all today, so adding one class of them there would be the only
  reason to touch it.
- **D-23** (stop computed per read) — a stop is set *after* publish, so materialising at publish would
  mean recomputing on every stop write anyway; the read is already behind ETag/CDN and
  `catalog_location_stop_version`.
- Layout details left open: how tabs behave when a group has many long names (scroll vs wrap), tile
  column count per breakpoint, placeholder iconography.

</decisions>

<requirement_deltas>
## Requirement deltas — REQUIREMENTS.md and the design note both diverge

Six of the twelve ING requirements changed during this discussion, and the design note has two
statements of fact that are wrong. Downstream agents must plan against this list, not against the
note or the unamended requirement text.

| Requirement | What changes |
|---|---|
| **ING-05** | Rewritten. There are no numeric selection rules. A group is display (tiles \| tabs) + behaviour (one \| several) + required (D-07). "Effective max counts distinct chosen options" no longer applies to anything. |
| **ING-06** | **Deferred to the backlog.** No default-in, no `amount: 0`, no "без лука" through modifiers (D-05). Removal moves to the composition (D-16). |
| **ING-07** | **Deferred to the backlog.** No stepper, no amounts above one (D-06). |
| **ING-08** | Rewritten. No cascade. A stopped ingredient greys out through `/v1/menu/availability`; dishes are stopped only by the operator, offered a checkbox list built from composition (D-20/D-21/D-22). |
| **ING-09** | Corrected. The published menu does not drop stopped things — it never has. The ingredient stop rides the availability response as a second list (D-21). The `modifierOptions[]` top-level dedupe and presigned `imageUrl` still stand. |
| **ING-11** | Rewritten. Display comes from an explicit group setting, not from whether any ingredient has a photo. Behaviour comes from an explicit group setting, not from effective max (D-07/D-08). |
| **ING-12** | Expanded well past a rename. Composition gains a second authoring mode built from ingredients, a per-line removable flag, guest visibility, and exclusions reaching the order (D-13 … D-19). |

**New requirements are needed** — assign ids at `/gsd-plan-phase` or amend REQUIREMENTS.md first:
1. A dish's composition can be assembled from library ingredients as an ordered list, or written as
   free text, the operator choosing the mode per dish.
2. An assembled composition line is marked removable or not; a removable line is shown to the guest
   with a cross and can be struck through.
3. A struck-through composition line reaches the order as its own rows with a name snapshot, never as
   comment text, and shows on the operator's order detail as a separate line.

**Note overrides** — `.planning/notes/ingredients.md` sections that are now superseded:
`## Selection rules`, `## Default-in, and taking it out`, `## Amounts above one`, the cascade
paragraph of `## Stop list`, the "omitted from the published menu" claim in the same section, and the
whole photo-driven table in `## Guest UI`.

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- The founder's framing of the composition, verbatim in substance: adding an ingredient to a
  composition sets one property — removable or not. Not removable reads as plain text. Removable shows
  the guest a cross; tapping strikes the line through and means "leave it out".
- "Библиотека" is a planning word only. The admin says **Ингредиенты**; the code keeps saying
  `modifier`.
- The pain being fixed, in the founder's own terms: bacon has to be created twice today — once inside
  the pizza group and once inside the burger group — with two photos and two prices to keep in step.
- Tabs must be physically incapable of holding two selections; that is what makes them the honest
  rendering for a choose-one block.

</specifics>

<deferred>
## Deferred Ideas

- **Disliked ingredients (own phase, founder's request).** Remember that a guest excludes onion, ask
  on the first exclusion whether to remember it, then check preferences whenever a dish is opened and
  warn (or highlight the line in the composition) when a disliked ingredient is in it. The founder's
  own words: "этот ux нужно хорошо продумать". Open questions it must answer: where preferences live
  for an anonymous guest, what happens when someone orders from another person's phone, and how the
  first-time question is phrased so it does not irritate. Related: `.planning/notes/guest-identity.md`.
- **Amounts and the stepper (ING-07).** `− n +` on a selected tile, `price_delta × max(0, amount −
  free_amount)`, per-attachment "how many times it can be added". Deferred out of 10.6.
- **Default-in ingredients (ING-06).** Pre-checked options and `amount: 0` reaching the kitchen as
  "без …". Superseded here by composition exclusions; revisit only if a real menu needs a pre-checked
  *modifier* rather than a removable composition line.
- **An exact numeric group minimum/maximum** ("choose exactly 2 sauces"). Nobody has asked; the two
  toggles cover every group in the current menu.
- **Price per size** — designed in `.planning/notes/modifier-pricing.md`.
- **Per-dish group rules** — iiko's min/max on the dish-to-group link; the link table can take the
  columns when a real menu needs them.
- **Assembly charts with grams and cost** — back-of-house, iiko's domain, needs stock first.
- **Nested modifiers** — iiko supports them; nobody has asked.
- **Search and paging in the ingredients list** — add when a tenant's list gets crowded (D-27).
