8. **Non-owner boundary.** Sign in as the location-1-scoped non-owner. Assert they can cancel a location-1 order, and that the detail Sheet's discretionary `Возврат` section is **absent from the DOM** (not merely disabled).
9. **Tab title.** After a new unaccepted order appears, assert `document.title` matches `(N) Заказы`, and that navigating away restores the base title.
