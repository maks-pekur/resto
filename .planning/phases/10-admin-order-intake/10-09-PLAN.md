<objective>
Ship the guest half of the phase: rewrite the checkout status tracker so it keeps polling past `paid` and renders the operator's progress in human wording, and add the marketing-consent checkbox with its write path.

Purpose: today `TERMINAL_STATUSES` includes `'paid'`, so polling stops the instant payment confirms and `accepted → preparing → ready` never render — the entire phase is invisible to the guest and there is nothing to demo to a prospective restaurant. Separately there is zero consent capture repo-wide, so Phase 12 CRM would build a mailing list from `orders.customer_email` with no lawful basis, and consent cannot be granted retroactively.

Output: a live two-screen demo loop and a GDPR-clean consent record, both fully localized.
</objective>
