<objective>
Remove the brand dimension from every remaining tenant-scoped table and from the tenant-aware database
client: nine `brand_id` columns across four schema files, their composite FKs, the unique indexes keyed on
`(tenant_id, brand_id, ...)`, the `brandId` field on `TenantContext`, and the `app_bind_brand` binding plus
its GUC drift sentinel in `withTenant`.

Purpose: this is the second half of the schema merge. Plan 03 defined the merged entity; this plan removes
the dangling references to the entity that no longer exists, so plan 05 can write one coherent migration.
Output: `packages/db` typechecks with zero `brand` identifiers outside historical migration SQL.
</objective>
