# Phase 10.2 Plan 08: Delete brand-scope guard and active-brand pin, rename surviving surfaces Summary

**The identity context's brand-scope guard, active-brand session pin, and their ports/adapters are deleted outright (D-10); the guard chain drops from five to four; and the surviving "list my organizations" / slug-availability surfaces are renamed and rewired to query `member`/`tenants` directly instead of the deleted brand-provisioning port.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~65 min
- **Tasks:** 2 of 2 committed
- **Files modified:** 26 (7 created, 7 modified, 12 deleted outright, 3 renamed with rewritten content, 4 test specs deleted)

## Accomplishments

- `BrandScopeGuard`, `SetActiveBrandService`/`.controller.ts`, `member-brand-scope-drizzle.reader.ts`, `initial-brand-drizzle.repository.ts`, `brand-provisioning.{port,adapter}.ts`, `session-active-brand-writer.port.ts`, `session-active-brand.adapter.ts`, `domain/brand-errors.ts` deleted with no shim, no `@deprecated` alias (D-10, D-39).
- Global guard chain reduced from five to four, order preserved: `AuthGuard -> PermissionsGuard -> LocationScopeGuard -> OwnerOnlyGuard`.
- `GET /v1/me/tenants` (the organizations a signed-in user belongs to, with `slug`/`displayName`/`status`) and `GET /v1/me/tenants/slug-availability` (still per-route rate-limited) exist and typecheck clean.
- `POST /v1/me/brands` and `POST /v1/me/set-active-brand` deleted with no replacement this phase, exactly as CONTEXT.md's deferred GAP and D-14 direct.
- `initial-location-drizzle.repository.ts` (the similarly-named survivor) untouched; `LocationScopeGuard`, `MEMBER_LOCATION_SCOPE_READER`, `SESSION_ACTIVE_LOCATION_WRITER` all intact.
- Identity `src/` typecheck errors: 56 -> 30, and every one of the 30 remaining errors lives in a file this plan does not own (D-41 `organizationId`/`organizationRole` renames — plan 21's territory — and the pre-existing, unowned `brands`/`brandId` breakage in `initial-location-drizzle.repository.ts`/`member-location-scope-drizzle.reader.ts`, present in the baseline before this plan touched anything).
