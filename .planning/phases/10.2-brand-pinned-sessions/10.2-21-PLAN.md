<objective>
Finish D-41: sweep the `organization` vocabulary out of our own code so the entity has exactly one name,
`tenant`, everywhere outside Better Auth's adapter directory.

Purpose: D-40 removed `brand`; this removes the second synonym. The founder's instruction is that
organization, brand and tenant were three names for one entity and only one should survive. Plan 05 already
renamed the database side (`tenant_role`, `tenant_id`, `active_tenant_id`) and told Better Auth about it via
its `schema` option. What remains is our own identifiers — roughly 100 files at the time this plan was
written, though plans 06–19 rewrite many of them first, which is exactly why this sweep runs last rather
than being smeared across eight plans.

Output: `rg -li "organization" apps packages tools` returns only paths under
`apps/api/src/contexts/identity/infrastructure/better-auth/`.
</objective>
