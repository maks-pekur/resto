# Phase 10.2 Plan 10: Sweep Ordering, Payments and Notifications Summary

**Ordering, payments and notifications now resolve every order, checkout and guest-notification identity from the merged `Tenant` aggregate instead of the deleted `Brand`/`brandId` dimension — connected-account lookup, checkout currency, the `account.updated` webhook, and the guest email sender identity all repointed at `TenantRepository`, with the ordering event contract dropping `brandId` outright.**

## Accomplishments

- `OrderSnapshot`, `CreateOrderInput`, `OrderCreatedDomainEvent`, `OrderFeedQuerySchema` and `MenuPricingPort.loadSnapshot` all drop `brandId` — an order is now scoped by `tenantId` + `locationId` only (D-08).

- Every repaired spec strengthens or preserves an assertion on an observable outcome (persisted `Tenant` snapshot fields, emitted envelope payload shape, rendered email `vars`/`locale`) rather than only a mock call — see Money-Path Resolution and Repaired Specs below.
