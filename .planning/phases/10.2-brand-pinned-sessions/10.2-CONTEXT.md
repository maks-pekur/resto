# Phase 10.2: Brand-pinned sessions and account onboarding - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-08-19
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Make one brand the fixed context of a session — chosen at sign-in, never switched
inside the app — and give a new owner a working path from "create account" to
"first brand", so every operator surface, guard and URL resolves against a single
brand for the life of the session.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Tenancy model (settled this discussion — foundational)

- **D-01:** The **tenant is the group**. It is the sharing boundary (staff, guests,
  loyalty, cross-brand analytics) and the subscription boundary. The **brand is the
  legal entity** inside it, carrying its own `legal_name`, `legal_form`, `tax_id`,
  `stripe_account_id` and country. `tenants → brands` stays as-is.
- **D-02:** Collapsing to `tenant = brand` (one legal entity per tenant, owner as a
  member of several tenants) was explicitly considered and **rejected**. Rationale:
  an owner running several restaurants wants shared staff, shared guest base and
  shared loyalty across them. Recorded because the collapse would have removed the
  brand-pin machinery entirely and halved this phase — a future reader will ask why
  it wasn't taken.
- **D-03:** The subscription sits on the owner's account, and the plan caps how many
  brands the group may have. **The cap is NOT enforced in this phase** — billing does
  not exist yet. See `<deferred>`.
- **D-04:** `tenants.display_name` is **derived from the first brand's name** at
  onboarding. It is never asked separately and never gets a placeholder value.

### Brand authority — where the active brand comes from

- **D-05:** **The session is the single source of truth.** The `/{brandSlug}` URL
  segment is **removed entirely**; admin URLs become `/orders`, `/menu/items`.
  This supersedes decision D-03 of phase 02-03 (brand-in-URL).
- **D-06:** **No compatibility shim for old `/{brandSlug}/...` URLs** — no redirect,
  no pin-vs-URL reconciliation, no transition code. The product is unlaunched and no
  real bookmarks exist. Old URLs simply stop resolving.
- **D-07:** **Do not reintroduce a brand cookie.** Phase 02-03 shipped a signed
  `resto.active_brand` cookie (HMAC + dedicated secret + four I/O sites) and it was
  retired. The brand belongs on the session row, which cannot be forged.

### Session lifecycle and brand switching

- **D-08:** **One session = one brand, for the life of that session.** Switching
  brands **revokes the current session and issues a new one**. The old token is dead
  immediately after the switch.
- **D-09:** Switching does **not** re-prompt for the password. The owner picks a
  brand; the server revokes and re-issues server-side.
  > **Refines an earlier answer in the same discussion.** The user first chose
  > "lightweight re-pick, session stays, only the pin changes", then restated it as
  > "each login to a brand should be a new session; on switch we revoke the current
  > one and create a new one". The later, stricter statement wins: same user-facing
  > feel (no password), stronger security property (no token replay across brands).
- **D-10:** The **brand picker appears at every sign-in** when the owner has more
  than one brand — a fresh session has no brand yet, so there is nothing to remember.
  Exactly one brand → picker skipped, straight to the dashboard. Staff always have
  exactly one brand (08.5 D-14 closed non-owner switching) and never see the picker.
- **D-11:** Mirror the existing staff pick-location interstitial rather than
  inventing a second pattern.
- **D-12:** `apps/admin/src/components/brand-switcher.tsx` is **deleted**.
  `location-switcher.tsx` stays and becomes the only in-app context control.

### Signup

- **D-13:** Signup asks **Name, Email, password, Country** — nothing else.
- **D-14:** The form must call **`/v1/signup`** (which provisions tenant + owner
  atomically), not Better Auth directly. Today it calls Better Auth and produces a
  user with **zero memberships** — a stranded account that cannot create a brand
  (verified live 2026-08-19).
- **D-15:** Remove the **dead currency field** (collected, never sent) and fix the
  mislabelled "Restaurant name" field (its value goes to Better Auth as the person's
  name).
- **D-16:** **Close direct Better Auth signup** (`POST /api/auth/sign-up/email`).
  Check invitation acceptance first — it may share the same path, and blindly
  disabling it would break invite redemption.

### Onboarding

- **D-17:** Onboarding is **creating the first brand**, and nothing else. The tenant
  is created silently at signup; the owner never sees a "restaurant name" step.
  > This corrects the shape sketched in ROADMAP.md ("restaurant name, then the first
  > brand"). The user's own framing: _"бренд это и есть компания, тенант это просто
  > связующее звено на котором будет подписка и общие параметры"_.
- **D-18:** The brand step asks **name only**. Slug is derived automatically.
  Legal details (`legal_name`, `legal_form`, `tax_id`) are **not** collected here —
  Stripe Connect Express collects them at payout setup, and asking twice is waste.
- **D-19:** Onboarding **resumes at the step where it was abandoned**, with the
  ability to go back and change the previous step. Derive the resume point from the
  data that exists, not from a separate progress flag.

### Countries, currency and language

- **D-20:** Supported countries are a **config in `packages/domain`**, alongside the
  other shared registries (`money.ts`, `rbac/`, `reserved-slugs.ts`) — no
  infrastructure imports, usable from api and both web apps. It is the seam every
  per-market setting lands on.
- **D-21:** First supported list: **UA, GB, ES**.
  > The user said "ua and en and es". `en` is not a country; the English-speaking
  > market was resolved to **GB** (not US) because Stripe Connect Express is
  > long-stable there and the currency question is unambiguous.
- **D-22:** **Country lives on the brand only**, not on the tenant. At signup it is
  collected and **carried into onboarding as the first brand's country** — it is not
  persisted on the tenant on the way through. No `country` column exists anywhere
  today; this phase adds it to `brands`.
- **D-23:** **Currency derives from country and is never asked.** UA→UAH, GB→GBP,
  ES→EUR. `brands.default_currency` already exists and is nullable (null = inherit
  from tenant). The Stripe adapter (`stripe-provider.adapter.ts`) already accepts
  `country` and `default_currency` as optional inputs and nothing supplies them —
  this finally feeds a wire already in place.
- **D-24:** **Interface language is a user-level choice, surfaced by a
  `langSwitcher` in the admin UI.** Its initial value derives from the signup
  country's default locale. Rationale (user's words): the language serves as both
  interface and communication language, and the person — not the venue — is who
  reads it. A cook who speaks Ukrainian in a Spanish restaurant keeps their language.
- **D-25:** The **Spanish (`es`) catalogue is added in this phase** for both admin
  and website, covering **interface and communication** (emails/notifications), not
  interface alone. Admin ships `ru`/`en` today; website ships `ru`/`uk`/`en`.
- **D-26:** A country whose default locale has no catalogue must **fall back
  deliberately** rather than render raw keys — the failure mode already seen in
  phase 10 when a component read a key from the wrong namespace.

### Code hygiene (standing instruction for this phase)

- **D-27:** **Delete, do not deprecate.** The user's instruction: _"при реализации
  выпиливай все лишние чтобы не тянулся старый ненужный код"_. Named dead code this
  phase must remove:
  - `apps/admin/src/components/brand-switcher.tsx` (per D-12)
  - `@RequireBrand` / `REQUIRE_BRAND_KEY` — present in 8 api files, **read by no
    guard**
  - the signup currency field (per D-15)
  - `AutoSaveIndicator` / `useDebouncedAutosave` — abandoned auto-save model
  - `friendlyCatalogError` in `apps/admin/src/lib/menu/catalog-errors.ts` — dead,
    English-only
  - `apps/admin/app/` — leftover Next.js directory, untracked

### Claude's Discretion

- Picker visual design (constrained only by D-11: mirror the location interstitial)
- How the resume point in D-19 is computed from existing data
- Slug derivation strategy for brands in D-18
- Where the `langSwitcher` sits in the admin chrome
- Mechanism for revoke-and-reissue in D-08 (Better Auth natively supports
  `setActiveOrganization` and multi-org membership; `activeOrganizationId` is
  already read in 8 api files)

</decisions>

## Specific Ideas

- _"бренд это и есть компания, тенант это просто как связующее звено на котором будет
  подписка и общие параметры, а бренд это конкретное ооо или ип со своим счетом"_ —
  the user's own model statement; the schema already matches it (`brands` carries
  `legal_name`, `legal_form`, `tax_id`, `stripe_account_id`, `fiscalization_config`).
- _"по сути каждый логин к бренду это должна быть новая сессия и при смене бренда мы
  должны отозвать текущую и создать новую"_ — the literal source of D-08.
- _"давай добавим langSwitcher в ui"_ — the literal source of D-24.
- The brand picker should feel like the staff pick-location interstitial that already
  exists, not a new pattern.

## Existing Code Insights

### Reusable Assets

- **Better Auth multi-org is native.** A user can belong to several organizations;
  `activeOrganizationId` lives on the session and `setActiveOrganization` is already
  called in `signup.service.ts`. Nothing new is needed in the auth layer to support a
  picker.
- **`/v1/signup` already does the right thing** — provisions tenant + owner
  atomically. The bug is only that the admin form does not call it.
- **The session row already carries `active_brand_id`** and `onInitialBrandPin` pins
  a brand at login when none is set. Missing: the picker, the switcher removal, and
  revoke-and-reissue.
- **The staff pick-location interstitial** is the pattern the brand picker mirrors.
- **The Stripe adapter already accepts `country` and `default_currency`** — the
  country config feeds an existing wire rather than building a new one.

### Established Patterns

- Six global guards run in order: `AuthGuard → PermissionsGuard → BrandScopeGuard →
LocationScopeGuard → OwnerOnlyGuard → RateLimitGuard`. Removing the URL segment
  changes `BrandScopeGuard`'s input, not its position.
- `brands.locale` / `brands.default_currency` are nullable and mean "inherit from
  tenant". The country column should follow the same nullable-inherit convention only
  if D-22 is later widened; as written, country is brand-only and required there.
- Shared registries in `packages/domain` are plain Zod + derived types with zero
  infrastructure imports.

### Integration Points

- Route tree restructure: 18 files under `$brandSlug`, 58 files referencing
  `brandSlug` across the admin app.
- `@RequireBrand` / `REQUIRE_BRAND_KEY` spans 8 api files and is read by no guard —
  removed under D-27.
- `x-brand-slug` header handling in `tenant-context.middleware.ts`.

### Known cost (from ROADMAP)

- `set-active-brand.e2e` and `brand-isolation.e2e` were brought onto the current
  contract on 2026-08-19 and encode brand-switching semantics — **both are rewritten
  by this phase**.
- `adm-00` scenarios 3, 6, 7a and 7b test the brand switcher, cross-tab brand sync and
  add-brand-from-switcher. They are currently `test.fixme`, deliberately left
  unrepaired pending this phase — they must be repaired or deleted here, not left.

<deferred>
## Deferred Ideas

- **Brand-count cap by subscription plan (D-03).** The rule is decided — the plan
  determines how many brands a group may have — but billing does not exist, so
  nothing enforces it. Belongs in the billing phase.
- **Owner-level subscription entity.** Where the subscription actually lives as data
  (on the tenant vs on the user) is a billing-phase decision; this phase only records
  that the plan governs brand count.
- **Shared staff / guests / loyalty across brands in the UI.** The model permits it
  (D-01) and it is the reason the group model was kept, but no surface exposes it yet.
- **Country config extensions:** timezone defaults, address shape, and the per-market
  fiscal rules deliberately deferred in CLAUDE.md Constraints. The config gains the
  seam (D-20); the fields come later.
- **US as a supported country.** GB was chosen over US for the English market (D-21);
  US adds state-level tax complexity and can be added to the config later without
  structural change.
- **Guest-facing email localisation beyond the languages named in D-25.**

</deferred>
