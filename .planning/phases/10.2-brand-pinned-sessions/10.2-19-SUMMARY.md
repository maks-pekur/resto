**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] `payments-isolation.e2e.spec.ts`'s `beforeAll` failed on 2 pre-existing NOT NULL violations, skipping all 5 tests**

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] `apps/admin/src/routes/(auth)/login.tsx`'s `expired` search param crashed on `/login?expired=1`**

- **Found during:** Job 2, scenario 6 design (the exact URL a stale session must reach)
- **Issue:** TanStack Router's default `parseSearch` coerces `?expired=1` to the number `1`; `z.string().optional()` then rejected it, falling to the generic `RouteError` boundary instead of rendering the session-expired banner. Flagged in `deferred-items.md` since plan 17, never fixed.
- **Fix:** `expired: z.union([z.string(), z.number()]).transform((v) => String(v)).optional()` — scoped to this one route's schema, not a router-wide `parseSearch` change (no other search param in the app collides with numeric coercion).
- **Files modified:** `apps/admin/src/routes/(auth)/login.tsx`
- **Verification:** live Playwright navigation to `http://admin.localhost:4000/login?expired=1` — status 200, `session-expired-notice` banner visible, no error-fallback text present.
- **Committed in:** `8d76d1d5`

**3. [Rule 3 - Blocking] `apps/admin/e2e/` had no locale-independent selector for the organization-identity/nav-user UI**

- **Found during:** Job 2, designing scenarios 3 and 6
- **Issue:** The app's i18next `fallbackLng` is `ru` and `playwright.config.ts`'s own `use.locale` is `ru-RU`; most translated copy (onboarding, pick-organization, nav-user dropdown items) renders in Russian under test, making English-text selectors unreliable. `tenant-identity.tsx` and `nav-user.tsx` had no `data-testid` at all (unlike the deleted brand-switcher, which had three).
- **Fix:** Added `data-testid="tenant-identity"`, `"nav-user-trigger"`, `"nav-switch-organization"`.
- **Files modified:** `apps/admin/src/components/tenant-identity.tsx`, `apps/admin/src/components/nav-user.tsx`
- **Verification:** scenarios 1/3/6 select on these testids and pass live.
- **Committed in:** `8d76d1d5`
