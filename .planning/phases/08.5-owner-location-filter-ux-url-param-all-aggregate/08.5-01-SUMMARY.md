## Accomplishments

- Backend owner-only authorization primitive exists, mirroring the codebase's existing `@Permissions`/`@LocationNeutral` opt-in metadata-guard convention exactly
- `OwnerOnlyGuard` throws a bare `NotFoundException()` (no `{code,message}` body) for any non-owner or non-operator principal when `@OwnerOnly()` is present, and no-ops (passes) when absent
- Proved `location-scope.guard.ts` is byte-for-byte unchanged (`git diff --stat` empty) and that the pre-existing staff-isolation e2e regression net (10/10 cases) stays green after adding the new guard to the chain
