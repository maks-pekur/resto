<objective>
Build the operator's floor screen at `/{locationSlug}/tables`: zones listing their tables, create-with-N-tables, add table, rename, archive, print the zone sheet, and download a single QR.

Purpose: this is the surface that makes everything else in the phase reachable by a human. It is deliberately a list and not a floor map — the map changes nothing about taking an order, and no coordinate columns exist to draw one from.

There is no UI design contract for this screen. Plan its structure and behaviour — route, queries, mutations, and the states it must survive — and do not specify pixel layout, spacing or component-level visual design. The founder expects to revise it once they have seen it.
Output: one route, its queries, two components, the sidebar entry, three locale files, and a spec covering the four states TBL-11 names.
</objective>
