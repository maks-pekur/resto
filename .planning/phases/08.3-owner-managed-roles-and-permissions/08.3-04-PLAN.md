<objective>
Ship the owner-facing admin UI for Phase 08.3: a Roles list page, a role editor driven by the
permission catalog with a presets-first create flow, and the minimal assignment surface on the
Team page — the Wave 4 front-end for the server-side role model built in plans 01/02/03. This is
the UI half of ROADMAP SC#1 ("owner creates a custom role in admin, selects its permissions from
the catalog, saves it") and SC#2 ("owner assigns a custom role to a staff member").

The UI consumes the plan-02 wrapper endpoints (`POST/PUT/DELETE/GET /v1/roles`) and the plan-03
assignment endpoints (`POST /v1/roles/:roleSlug/assign`, `GET /v1/roles/members`). It enforces no
authority of its own: owner-gating, hiding NON_DELEGATABLE toggles, and disabling self-assignment
are UX conveniences — the server is the sole authority (D-01/D-04/D-06/D-14, plans 01-03). Every
server rejection (subset violation, self-escalation, NON_DELEGATABLE, occupied role, reserved
name, role cap) is surfaced to the operator with the exact UI-SPEC copy.

Purpose: Make the server-side role feature usable end-to-end by a real owner in the admin panel.
Output: `roles.ts` query bindings, two routes (`roles.tsx`, `roles.$roleId.tsx`), the Team page
extension, five new components (RoleList, RoleForm, PermissionCatalog, PresetPicker,
MemberRoleRow), a sidebar link, and route-tree wiring in `main.tsx`.
</objective>
