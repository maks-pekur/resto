<objective>
Prove — at the running HTTP stack, not only in unit isolation — the three security invariants that
justify enabling `dynamicAccessControl` in this phase. Plans 01-03 built the guards and covered them
with unit specs; the plan-checker (and the phase's own `Security review: REQUIRED` gate) demands an
integration proof that the guards actually reject at the boundary. This plan holds exactly three e2e
specs:

1. **Privilege-escalation proof (D-06 / SC#3 / D-04)** — the escalation surface that kept
   `dynamicAccessControl` disabled. Proves: (a) an admin-tier principal cannot create a role at all
   (owner-only `@Permissions({ac:['create']})` → 403); (b) even the owner cannot mint a
   NON_DELEGATABLE-bearing role (`containsNonDelegatable` → 403, D-04 holds for owner too); (c) an
   assigner cannot assign a role stronger than they hold or a NON_DELEGATABLE-bearing role via the
   assignment endpoint (assignment-subset guard, D-06 / CTO C-1).
2. **Brand-scope orthogonality proof (D-03 / RBAC-09)** — a custom role grants tenant-wide
   permissions but does NOT widen brand reach: a `{ menu: ['update'] }` custom-role member scoped to
   Brand A writes in Brand A (200) and gets existence-hiding 404 in Brand B.
3. **Cross-tenant isolation proof (RBAC-14 / HIGH-7)** — a Tenant A session cannot read or write
   Tenant B's `organization_role` records through the `/v1/roles` wrapper, because `organizationId`
   is ALS-pinned, never client-supplied.

This plan is a **security-regression test plan** — it writes no production code. It depends on the
finished server surfaces from plans 01/02/03 (`RolesController`, `MemberRolesController`,
`CreateRoleService` NON_DELEGATABLE guard, `AssignRoleService` subset guard, the `ac` resource and
owner-only grant). The D-03 test also depends on 08.2's `member_brand_scope` + `BrandScopeGuard`,
already shipped.

Purpose: Convert "the guards exist and unit-pass" into "the guards reject at the HTTP boundary,
proven end-to-end" — discharging SC#3, D-03, D-04-on-owner, and the cross-tenant isolation net.
Output: three Docker-gated e2e specs mirroring the existing `apps/api/test/e2e` harness.
</objective>
