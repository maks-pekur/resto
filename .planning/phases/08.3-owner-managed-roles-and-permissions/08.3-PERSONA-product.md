# Phase 08.3 — Product Strategist Review: Owner-managed Roles & Permissions

**Reviewer lens:** Head of Product / Product Strategist (restaurant-tech vertical)
**Phase:** 08.3 Owner-managed Roles & Permissions (`/gsd-discuss-phase` pass)
**Date:** 2026-07-04

---

## Product Take (one paragraph)

The founder calls custom roles "the headline access feature," but for the target customer
(independent restaurants + small networks, 1–10 locations, EU/EN — `.planning/PROJECT.md`
"Target customer") it is **table-stakes plumbing, not a wow moment**. A first customer has
1 brand and a handful of staff; they think in **job titles** ("менеджер смены", "кассир",
"повар", "маркетолог"), never in `menu:update` / `order:update-status`
(`packages/domain/src/rbac/permissions.ts`). The single biggest product risk here is
shipping a **from-scratch permission-matrix builder as the first (and only) experience** —
a classic enterprise-UX trap that maximizes confusion and time-to-value at exactly the
moment (first non-owner hire) when the owner just wants "give my manager access, but not
the money stuff." The right MVP is **2–3 sensible preset roles that work out of the box**,
with custom roles as a **clone-and-tweak power-user path** — which is exactly how Toast's
"Jobs" model works ([Toast job roles](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Creating-and-Editing-Job-Roles)).
There is also a **hard end-to-end blocker**: SC#2 requires "assign a custom role to a staff
member," but the only place to assign roles — a real team-management page — is **deferred to
Phase 17** (`.planning/ROADMAP.md` Phase 17). Today's team page
(`apps/admin/src/routes/(protected)/$brandSlug/team.tsx`) is invite-only and shows *pending
invitations* — there is **no active-member list to assign a role on**. Without closing that
gap the feature is not usable end-to-end and SC#2 cannot pass. Finally, the phase should be
weighed against the revenue spine: **Phase 10 (Admin Order Intake) is the last MVP-1 gate
before "restaurant sees paid orders"** and is *Not started* (`.planning/ROADMAP.md` MVP-1
Phase Status). Custom roles do not move the first-paying-customer needle; scope this tight
and do not let it displace Phase 10.

## Findings

### HIGH

#### H-1 — End-to-end gap: SC#2 (assign-to-staff) has nowhere to happen. The team page is invite-only, no member list, and role-assignment UI is deferred to Phase 17.
**Evidence:** `apps/admin/src/routes/(protected)/$brandSlug/team.tsx` renders only *pending
invitations* + an invite button; there is no active-member list and no per-member role
control. Role-change UI (`auth.api.updateMemberRole`, member list, in-place role change) is
explicitly **Phase 17 / TEAM-01..03** (`.planning/ROADMAP.md` Phase 17 SC#1/#3), which is
trigger-gated on "first paying tenant adds a 2nd member with role ≠ owner." SC#2 of this
phase ("Owner assigns a custom role to a staff member; effective permissions reflect it on
the next request") therefore has **no surface to run on** — you can create a role and never
attach it to a human.
**Consequence:** The headline feature is a dead-end demo: owner builds a beautiful custom
role, then hits a wall. Feature looks broken/unfinished on first contact — the worst
first-impression outcome for the "headline" feature.
**Recommendation:** Pull the **minimum assignment slice into 08.3**: an active-member list on
the existing brand `team` page with a per-member **role dropdown** (system roles + this
tenant's custom roles), wired to `auth.api.updateMemberRole`. This is the Phase-17 TEAM-03
slice, but SC#2 forces it here — do not ship 08.3 without it. Keep the *rest* of Phase 17
(pending-invite revoke, 2FA subordinate reset, recovery-code regen) deferred. Flag the
overlap explicitly so Phase 17 shrinks rather than double-builds.

#### H-2 — Wrong first experience: a raw permission matrix instead of named preset roles. Owners hire "a manager," not a bag of 20+ resource:action toggles.
**Evidence:** The catalog is 8 resources × 2–4 actions = ~20 permissions
(`packages/domain/src/rbac/permissions.ts`: menu/order/staff/reports/settings/billing/tenant/
brand). SC#1 as drafted ("selects its permissions from the catalog") implies a from-scratch
matrix as the primary path. `.planning/PROJECT.md` "Target customer" is 1–10 locations,
mid-market independents — not enterprise ops teams who staff a permissions admin. Competitor
norm (Toast "Jobs": Server / Cashier / Manager / Bartender / Cook, permissions inherited per
job — [Toast](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Creating-and-Editing-Job-Roles)) leads
with **named templates**, not a matrix.
**Consequence:** Time-to-value tanks. The owner's actual job ("let my manager in, keep them
out of billing/payouts") becomes a 20-checkbox decision they are unqualified to make, so
they either over-grant (security risk) or bounce.
**Recommendation:** Lead with **2–3 seeded, ready-to-use preset roles** beyond the 3 system
roles — concretely **"Manager"**, **"Cashier / Front-of-house"**, **"Kitchen"** — each a
named bundle the owner can assign *immediately with zero configuration*. Custom-role creation
becomes **"Duplicate this role and tweak,"** not "start from an empty grid." The empty-matrix
builder is the power-user escape hatch, not the front door. (See "Recommended MVP shape.")

#### H-3 — Scope/sequencing: 08.3 must not displace Phase 10 (revenue spine). Custom roles do not move the first-paying-customer gate.
**Evidence:** MVP-1's gate is "first paying restaurant takes paid orders end-to-end"
(`.planning/ROADMAP.md` Overview). Phase 10 (Admin Order Intake — "the operator must see paid
orders") is **Not started**; Phase 7.5 (prod deploy) is 6/11 In Progress
(`.planning/ROADMAP.md` MVP-1 Phase Status). `.planning/PROJECT.md` Out-of-Scope even says
"**Full RBAC customization in v1** — three system roles cover MVP; per-tenant custom roles
defer to post-MVP." A first customer with one brand and 2–3 staff is fully served by
owner/admin/staff.
**Consequence:** Spending a UI-heavy phase on a power-user access feature before the operator
can even *see an order* inverts the value order. Risk: the "headline" framing pulls solo-founder
weeks away from the money path.
**Recommendation:** Keep 08.3 **deliberately thin** (presets + clone + assign; H-1/H-2 shape).
Sequence it to **not block Phase 10**; if there is contention, Phase 10 wins. Re-confirm with
the founder that 08.3 is intentionally ahead of Phase 10, given PROJECT.md deferred custom
roles post-MVP. This is a founder decision (see Top-3).

### MEDIUM

#### M-1 — Progressive disclosure: a flat 20-checkbox grid will confuse. Group by resource, use tri-state section toggles, and hide the dangerous permissions.
**Evidence:** `PERMISSIONS_STATEMENT` has natural resource groups already
(menu/order/staff/reports/settings/billing/tenant/brand). Some are owner-only landmines:
`tenant:delete`, `tenant:transfer`, `billing:update` — granting these to a custom role is a
foot-gun. The creator-subset guard (SC#3) stops *escalation*, but not an owner accidentally
handing "delete the whole company" to a shift manager.
**Recommendation:** Render permissions **grouped by resource, collapsed by default**, with a
per-group master toggle (All / Some / None) and a one-line plain-language summary per group
("Menu — edit items, prices, publish"). **Suppress or lock `tenant:delete`/`tenant:transfer`
from custom roles entirely** (they belong to owner only); gate `billing:*` and payouts behind
a "Sensitive" section with an explicit expand. Use plain labels, not `resource:action` slugs,
in the UI. Note: domain CLAUDE.md warns action names must not contain `:` if ever serialized —
keep the UI label layer decoupled from the wire slug.

#### M-2 — Mental model: owners need "who is on this role" (member count + faces), not just a role definition.
**Evidence:** The current model is invitation-centric (`team.tsx` shows invitations, not
memberships). SEED-001 and the founder's vision are "create role → toggle perms → **assign to
staff**" (`08.2-CONTEXT.md` Specific Ideas) — assignment is half the feature. The owner's real
question is "who can currently do X?" not "does role R contain permission P?".
**Recommendation:** On the roles list, show a **member count per role** ("Manager · 3 people")
and let the owner drill into the members on a role. This is the reverse index of H-1's
per-member dropdown and it is what makes the feature feel *managed* rather than *configured*.
If member-count is expensive this cycle, at minimum show it on the role detail view.

#### M-3 — Editing & blast radius: changing a role changes everyone on it, silently. Owners will not expect that.
**Evidence:** Dynamic RBAC means permissions resolve at request time
(`permission-checker.adapter.ts` calls `auth.api.hasPermission` live). Editing "Manager" to
remove `order:update-status` instantly demotes every manager mid-shift.
**Recommendation:** On save of an *edit* to an assigned role, surface a lightweight confirm:
"This changes access for N people using this role." Cheap to build, prevents a support ticket
class ("why did my whole team lose access?"). Also decide/display what happens to a member
when their **custom role is deleted** — fall back to `staff` (least privilege) and tell the
owner, never orphan them into undefined access.

#### M-4 — Naming & uniqueness rules are unspecified and will bite. Owners will type "Manager," "manager," "Менеджер."
**Evidence:** No stated constraint on custom-role name casing/uniqueness/reserved collisions
with system-role slugs (owner/admin/staff). BA persists custom roles in
`organization_role` per-tenant (`system-roles.ts` header comment).
**Recommendation:** Enforce per-tenant case-insensitive unique display names; **reserve the
three system-role slugs** (owner/admin/staff) so a custom "Owner" can't shadow the system one;
allow a display name distinct from the wire slug (owner types "Менеджер смены," slug is
`shift-manager`). Cap length (domain rule: free-text max). This is a small backend guard that
prevents confusing duplicates.

### LOW

#### L-1 — Positioning: custom roles are table-stakes vs Toast/Square/Lightspeed, not a differentiator. Don't market it as the headline.
**Evidence:** Toast leads with named "Jobs" and inherited permissions as a standard feature
([Toast](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Creating-and-Editing-Job-Roles)); Square and
Lightspeed have permission sets too. RestOS's stated moat is **AI-driven** + EU-first +
no-POS-lock-in (`.planning/PROJECT.md` "Positioning relative to alternatives"), not RBAC depth.
**Recommendation:** Internally, treat this as **hygiene that removes a sales objection** ("can I
limit what my staff sees?" → "yes, out of the box"), not a headline. The *headline* version of
this feature is MVP-2: **"tell the AI assistant 'let Anna manage the menu but not payouts' and
it configures the role."** Keep the data model clean now so the AI layer can drive it later.

#### L-2 — Copy/i18n: the UI must speak job-role language in RU + EN, not permission jargon.
**Evidence:** i18n exists (`apps/admin/src/lib/i18n/messages`); MEMORY notes RestOS
conversations need plain Russian and to hide infra decisions, surface only product/UX ones.
**Recommendation:** Preset role names and permission-group labels get first-class RU + EN
translations framed as **jobs and capabilities** ("Управление меню," "Приём заказов"), never
raw slugs. Ship the RU labels as a first-class locale, not an afterthought.

#### L-3 — Discoverability: where does "Roles" live in the brand-first IA? Don't bury it, don't over-elevate it.
**Evidence:** Brand-first IA under `/{brand}/...`; team/settings are brand-neutral-in-copy but
tenant-scoped-in-data (`08.2-CONTEXT.md` D-05). Roles are tenant-scoped like team.
**Recommendation:** Put Roles **adjacent to Team** (e.g., a tab on the Team/People page, or
Settings → Roles), consistent with the tenant-scoped-data reality. Keep copy brand-neutral so
it doesn't imply per-brand roles (they aren't, yet). Do not create a top-level sidebar entry
for a feature a 3-person restaurant touches twice a year.

## Recommended MVP shape for the role UI

A **presets-first, clone-to-customize** model. Front door is "pick a job," not "build a matrix."

1. **Seed 2–3 preset roles beyond the system roles**, ready to assign with zero config:
   - **Manager** — everything except `billing:*`, `tenant:*`, and `staff:remove` of others
     (tune with founder). "Runs the restaurant, not the company."
   - **Cashier / Front-of-house** — `menu:read`, `order:read`, `order:update-status`,
     `brand:read`. "Takes and moves orders; can't change the menu or money."
   - **Kitchen** — `menu:read`, `order:read`, `order:update-status`. "Sees orders, marks them
     ready."
   These are named bundles over the existing `PERMISSIONS_STATEMENT`; no new primitives needed.

2. **Roles list page** (near Team): system roles + presets + any custom roles, each with a
   **member count** (M-2) and a **Duplicate** action. Owner's default path never touches a
   checkbox: assign a preset and move on.

3. **Custom role = "Duplicate & tweak,"** not empty grid (H-2). The editor shows permissions
   **grouped by resource, collapsed, plain-language labels, tri-state group toggle** (M-1),
   with `tenant:delete`/`tenant:transfer` **not offered** and `billing:*`/payouts behind a
   "Sensitive" expand. Creator-subset guard (SC#3) enforced server-side and reflected in the UI
   by graying out permissions the creator lacks (don't silently drop them — show *why*).

4. **Assignment lives on the Team page** (H-1): active-member list with a per-member **role
   dropdown** (system + custom), wired to `auth.api.updateMemberRole`; effective permissions on
   next request (SC#2). Edit-blast-radius confirm (M-3) on saving an assigned role; delete a
   role → members fall back to `staff` with a notice.

5. **Naming guard** (M-4): per-tenant case-insensitive unique display names, system-slug
   reserved, RU/EN labels (L-2).

**What stays out of 08.3:** the full Phase-17 team page (pending-invite revoke, 2FA subordinate
reset, recovery-code regen), location-scoped roles (Phase 08.4), and any AI role-authoring
(MVP-2). Ship the narrowest thing that makes SC#1–#4 true end-to-end.

## Top 3 product decisions the founder must make

1. **Presets-first or matrix-first?** (Recommend presets-first.) Do we seed 2–3 ready-to-use
   preset roles (Manager / Cashier / Kitchen) so the common case is zero-config, and make the
   from-scratch matrix the power-user path — or is the from-scratch builder the primary SC#1
   experience? This decision reshapes the whole UI and directly drives time-to-value. (H-2)

2. **Do we pull the minimum team-management assignment slice into 08.3?** SC#2 (assign-to-staff)
   is unbuildable without an active-member list + per-member role dropdown, which today lives in
   trigger-gated Phase 17. Either 08.3 absorbs that thin slice (recommended — otherwise the
   headline feature dead-ends) **or** SC#2 is descoped to "assignment ships in Phase 17" and
   08.3 delivers role *authoring* only (a demo without a payoff). Can't have SC#2 as written
   without a decision here. (H-1)

3. **Where does 08.3 sit relative to Phase 10 (revenue spine)?** PROJECT.md explicitly deferred
   custom roles post-MVP; the first-paying gate is Phase 10 (operator sees paid orders), which
   is Not started. Confirm 08.3 is intentionally sequenced ahead of Phase 10, and that it's
   scoped thin enough not to steal weeks from the money path — or reorder so Phase 10 ships
   first and 08.3 follows. (H-3)
