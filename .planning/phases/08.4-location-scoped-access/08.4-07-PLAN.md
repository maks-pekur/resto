<objective>
Make per-location roles operative: an owner-only `AssignLocationRoleService` that upserts `member_location_scope.role` (accepting system OR custom slugs, enforcing 08.3's NON_DELEGATABLE guard for custom roles) and a `LocationPermissionChecker` that resolves a non-owner operator's effective permissions from their role at the active location — bypassing Better Auth's tenant-wide `hasPermission`, which cannot see location scope.

Purpose: D-06 (role per location), D-07 (08.3 guards apply), D-08 (effective permissions = role at active location). This is a privilege surface — the highest-risk correctness area of the phase.
Output: The assignment service + endpoint, the location permission checker wired for location-scoped routes, and unit proofs that over-granting is prevented.
</objective>
