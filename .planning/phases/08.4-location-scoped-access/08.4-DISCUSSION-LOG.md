## Session / switcher model (founder-designed, freeform → confirmed)

**Owner:** works within one brand at a time; cross-brand via a global modal switcher; within a brand sees data both brand-global (all locations) and per-location via a `locationSwitcher`; all switches silent (no re-login).
**Staff:** logs in to one location they have access to (picks at login if several); sees only that location within their role; switch = re-login; one location at a time; (future) open shift only in one location at a time.
**Confirmed:** Yes — "Да, так." Noted this is stricter than 08.2 D-13 (staff login-pinned vs silent re-pin); silent re-pin stays owner-only; security review reconciles.
