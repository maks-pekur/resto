## Decisions Made

- **`Location.archive()` throws `LocationAlreadyArchivedError` on re-archive** — the plan's acceptance criteria didn't require this, but `Tenant.archive()` (the closest analog `PATTERNS.md` names for the archive shape) does exactly this; kept the mirror faithful rather than silently no-op.
- **`countScopedMembers` counts all rows for the location**, not a "would lose ALL access" subset — the port signature takes only `locationId`, and RESEARCH.md Focus Area 4 frames it as "count scoped members before archiving," not a cross-location analysis. Simpler, defensible, and matches what the confirmation dialog needs ("N staff scoped to this location").
- **No `:slug` in the controller URL** — brand is resolved from ALS context (the `x-brand-slug` header path already wired by `TenantContextMiddleware`), matching `catalog.controller.ts`'s established brand-scoped pattern rather than `brand-onboarding.controller.ts`'s per-brand-in-URL pattern. This also lets the application services read `requireBrandContext()` directly, keeping the controller DTO free of any tenant/brand field.
- **`LOCATION_REPOSITORY` and the 3 new services are exported from `tenancy.module.ts`**, consistent with `BRAND_REPOSITORY`/`ProvisionBrandService` — anticipates later 08.4 plans (05/06/09) needing to inject them from other contexts.
