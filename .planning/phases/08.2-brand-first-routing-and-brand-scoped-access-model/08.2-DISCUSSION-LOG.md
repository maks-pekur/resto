## Initial scope (FULL vs split)

Founder originally chose "everything" (routing + full server-enforced access model) at phase
promotion. During discussion this was reconsidered after persona reviews + the freeform answer
clarifying the real goal (limit staff to one/several locations; multi-brand membership; one
active brand per session; owner/admin full access; skepticism of cookies; interest in RBAC).

**Persona input (08.2-PERSONA-REVIEWS.md):** ~70% of the "server-enforced access model"
already ships (BrandScopeGuard, server-side `x-brand-slug`, `auth.tenant_mismatch`,
reserved-slugs). No `locations` table exists. The real delta = default-allow→deny flip +
server-session active-brand pin + (optional) brand RLS. Both personas recommended cutting
locations (YAGNI for a single-owner first customer) and the "re-login to switch" friction.

---

## Owner-authored roles (new requirement raised mid-discussion)

Founder: "owner creates roles, configures access for them, assigns roles to staff."
Verified in code: this is exactly better-auth `dynamicAccessControl`, **already integrated but
disabled** at `auth.config.ts:206` ("until tenant role-creation enforces a creator-subset
permission check"). Primitives (`PERMISSIONS_STATEMENT`, `SYSTEM_ROLES`, permission-checker)
already exist. Conclusion: a real but well-scoped feature → split into its own follow-on phase.
