<objective>
Reshape the Stripe Connect linkage from the tenant aggregate to the brand aggregate (D-04/D-05/D-06). Purpose: This is the schema + domain foundation every later plan in this phase builds on. Per-brand currency revises Phase 8 D-05; per-brand account resolution is the precondition for repointing checkout/refund/webhook (Plan 03) and onboarding (Plans 04/05).
Output: Migration 0057, reshaped `brands` schema, extended `Brand` aggregate, `findByStripeAccountId` on the brand repo, and a Stripe-free `Tenant` aggregate.
</objective>
